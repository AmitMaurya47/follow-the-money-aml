# Fan-out and fan-in (smurfing) typologies

Smurfing splits large sums into many small transfers, or gathers many small transfers into one place, to stay below reporting thresholds and avoid attention.

## Fan-out (dispersal)
One account sends funds to many distinct counterparties in a short period. A high number of distinct outbound partners relative to transaction count (partner diversity) is the key indicator. Check whether the recipients are newly created accounts or have little other activity.

## Fan-in (collection)
Many distinct senders transfer into one account, which then moves the consolidated amount elsewhere. Collection accounts often receive small, similar amounts from unrelated parties. Escalate when collection is followed by a single large outbound transfer.

## Structuring
Transfers are kept just below a reporting threshold, or are split across several days or institutions. Repeated amounts clustered below a round number are a red flag even when each transaction looks ordinary alone.
