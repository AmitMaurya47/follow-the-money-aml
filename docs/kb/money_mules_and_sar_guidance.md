# Money mules and case documentation

## Money mule accounts
A money mule is an account used to move illicit funds on behalf of others, often knowingly or through deception. Warning signs include a newly opened account with a sudden spike in volume, incoming funds from many unrelated parties, and quick withdrawal or onward transfer of most of the balance.

## Writing a case narrative
A good narrative states who is involved, what activity was observed, the time period, and the amounts. It separates facts from inference, cites the evidence behind each claim, and states why the activity is unusual for this customer. It does not allege a crime; it describes suspicious behaviour and the reasons for review.

## Escalation and human review
Automated scores support a decision but do not replace it. Every escalated case needs a human analyst to confirm the evidence, check for legitimate explanations, and decide whether a Suspicious Activity Report should be filed under the applicable local rules.
